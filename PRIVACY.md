# LightView Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

LightView is a native image viewer for macOS. It processes images and related metadata locally on your Mac.

## Data collection

LightView does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does not include accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking, or remote image services.

## File access

LightView accesses only files and folders that you explicitly open or authorize through macOS. The App Store edition uses Apple's App Sandbox and security-scoped permissions. Image decoding, EXIF inspection, slideshows, and MP4 export are performed on the device.

## Network access

LightView does not require network access for product functionality and does not upload images or metadata.

## Third-party components

LightView includes local, decoder-only components for SVG and WebP support. These components do not transmit data. License details are available in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` in the source repository.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be published in this repository.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at <https://github.com/longbai/lightview/issues>.
